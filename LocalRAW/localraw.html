What LocalRAW Does

🎯
Focus Check

Scores every photo of a shoot in one pass and sorts it into In focus, Fixable or Rejected. On people it judges the faces, so a sharp background never hides a soft face.

✋
You have the last word

Disagree with Focus Check? Press G, Y or R, or right-click a thumbnail, to move a photo to another group. Your choice stays, even when you check the folder again.

⭐
Stars and best of burst

Rate with keys 1–5 and show only 3★ and up. In a fast series, LocalRAW points out the sharpest frame of each burst.

🌡️
White balance that thinks

Pick Neutral on a white jersey or a grey wall, or let Auto Neutral find the light by itself. It refuses when unsure and checks that skin stays skin.

🎨
Tone and detail

Exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, white and black points, saturation, sharpening and dehaze — with Auto Tone as a starting point.

🔇
Noise reduction for high ISO

Measures the noise of each photo, so the same setting works at ISO 400 and ISO 12800.

✂️
Crop and straighten

Free, original, square, 4:5, 3:2, 16:9, 9:16 and more. Straighten a tilted horizon or field line.

🔎
Loupe, peaking, zoom

See any spot at 200 %, paint the sharpest edges red, or click to view at real size.

⤵️
Same edit, next photo

Keep edit carries your look to every new photo; Last Edit copies it in one click.

💾
Save and Export

Save writes a full-size JPEG copy into an Edited folder. Export, Lightroom-style: choose photos, size, quality, name, file-size limit and metadata.

🛡️
Originals never touched

Every edit is stored beside your photos. Your RAW and JPG files are never changed, and Trash All Rejected never throws away a photo you rated or edited.

📷
RAW and JPG

CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, DNG, RAF, ORF and more, plus JPG, PNG and TIFF.
