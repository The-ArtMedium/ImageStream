Satdiva / ImageStream LocalClip Privacy Policy Effective October 16, 2026 · Android application org.satdiva.localclip LocalClip is a free, offline video trimming application. It processes the videos you select locally on your Android device and does not upload them to the developer. Information collected and shared LocalClip does not collect, transmit, or share your personal information, selected videos, video clips, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking data with Satdiva / ImageStream or third parties. The app does not require an account, does not show advertisements, and does not use analytics or tracking services. Videos and on-device processing When you select a video to trim, LocalClip accesses the selected video on your device to preview and create your clips. Its media processing takes place on your device. LocalClip does not send your videos to a server. If you independently choose to share an exported clip using another app or online service, that action is under your control. The destination service's privacy practices then apply. Storage, retention and deletion LocalClip saves exported clips locally in the Movies/LocalClip folder. Your original videos and saved clips remain on your device until you delete them using your device's files, gallery, or media management tools. Exported clips may remain after LocalClip is uninstalled. Satdiva / ImageStream does not hold server copies of videos or clips. Data security LocalClip does not transfer the video content you select to Satdiva / ImageStream. Locally stored files are protected by the security features and settings of your Android device. Optional creator support LocalClip may provide an optional way to visit the creator's Ko-fi page. If you choose to open Ko-fi in a browser or another app, that external service may process information according to its own privacy policy. Supporting the creator is optional and is not required to use LocalClip. Children's privacy LocalClip does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. Changes to this policy If the app's data practices change, we will update this privacy policy and its effective date.
